Bullet Journal
==============

A personal bullet journal that runs on this computer only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Starting it
-----------
1. Keep this whole folder together (Bullet Journal.exe needs the _internal folder next to it).
   Put it somewhere permanent, e.g. Documents\Bullet Journal.
2. Double-click "Bullet Journal.exe". Your browser opens the journal.
   The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed:
   click "More info", then "Run anyway". If your work laptop blocks it outright, ask IT.
3. There is no window: the journal runs quietly in the background.
   To stop it: Settings > Stop the journal > Quit journal.
   To open it again: double-click Bullet Journal.exe (it opens the one already running).

Your data
---------
Everything is in the "data" folder created next to Bullet Journal.exe:
  data\journal.sqlite   your journal (one file; copy it to back up)
  data\backups\         a copy is made automatically each day (last 14 kept)
  data\journal.log      messages, if something goes wrong

Settings
--------
Start with Windows, working days, leave and meeting categories are in the app's Settings.
For Outlook meetings, copy config.example.toml to config.toml (same folder), open it in
Notepad and set calendar_path. "Outlook calendar set-up.md" in this folder explains the
Power Automate flow that exports your meetings.

Updating
--------
Quit the journal, then replace this folder's contents with the new version
EXCEPT the "data" folder and config.toml, which hold your journal and settings.
